# The Mortar

The **Mortar & Pestle** is an interactive crafting station utilized for processing and pulverizing materials into intermediate components, dusts, and compounds.

![Mortar Crafting Recipe](images/recipe_mortar_crafting.png)

## Mechanics

Unlike standard crafting stations, the Mortar requires manual player interaction over time.

1. **Input:** The Mortar accepts between 1 to 3 specific item inputs depending on the recipe. Right-click with items in hand to place them into the bowl.
2. **Processing:** Players must repeatedly right-click the block to advance the pestle's grinding progress.
3. **Speed Requirements:** Each recipe mandates a specific clicking rhythm:
   * **Slow:** Requires waiting briefly between clicks.
   * **Fast:** Requires rapid successive clicks.
   * **Any:** The recipe progresses regardless of click tempo.

> [!WARNING]
> Failing to maintain the required click speed can trigger failure conditions. Volatile recipes (such as Gunpowder) will explode violently if ground too rapidly!
