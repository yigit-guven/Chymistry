# Blast-Proof Cement

**Blast-Proof Cement** is an ultra-dense, explosion-immune construction block and emergency tactical defusal tool.

## Properties

* **Explosion Immunity**: Possesses a blast resistance of **1200.0F**, making it entirely impervious to TNT, Creepers, End Crystals, Respawn Anchors, and the Wither.
* **Mining**: Requires a Pickaxe to harvest.

---

## Active Defusal Mechanics

Carrying Blast-Proof Cement allows players to neutralize primed explosives and hostile explosive mobs:

### Defusing Primed TNT
* **Right-Click** any active Primed TNT entity with Blast-Proof Cement in hand.
* The lit fuse is extinguished instantly, converting the explosive back into a safe dropped **TNT item**.
* Consumes 1x Blast-Proof Cement.

### Defusing Creepers
* **Right-Click** a hissing/swelling Creeper with Blast-Proof Cement in hand.
* Instantly suppresses the Creeper's fuse, resetting its swelling counter and emitting slime and smoke particles.
* Consumes 1x Blast-Proof Cement.

---

## Crafting & Recipes

Blast-Proof Cement can be synthesized in multiple ways:

1. **[Crucible](Crucible) Slurry Synthesis**:
   * 1x [Quicklime](Quicklime) + 1x Sand + 1x Gravel + 1x Water Bucket ➔ **2x Blast-Proof Cement** (15 to 90 Heat, 10s). Returns empty bucket.
2. **Bulk Crafting**:
   * 4x Quicklime + 2x Sand + 2x Gravel + 1x Water Bucket in crafting table ➔ **4x Blast-Proof Cement**.
