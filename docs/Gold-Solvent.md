# Gold Solvent

**Item ID:** `chymistry:gold_solvent_bottle`

**Gold Solvent** is a fuming alchemical mixture of nitric and hydrochloric acid (analogous to *Aqua Regia*) engineered to dissolve noble metals.

## Uses

* **[Purified Gold Dust](Purified-Gold-Dust)**: Reacted with Raw Gold inside a heated **[Crucible](Crucible)** (20 to 1500 Heat) to extract 2x Purified Gold Dust.
