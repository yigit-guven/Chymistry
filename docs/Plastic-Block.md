# Plastic Block

**Plastic Block** is a lightweight, shock-absorbing synthetic polymer construction block.

## Properties

* **Resilience**: Engineered with high blast resistance (1200.0F), functioning as clean, non-conductive structural paneling.
* **Audio**: Emits dense wood-like stepping sounds.

---

## Manufacturing

1. **Synthesize Pellets**:
   * Synthesize **[Plastic Pellets](Plastic-Pellets)** in a **[Crucible](Crucible)** by reacting 1x Coal with 1x [Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid) between 25 to 120 Heat.
2. **Thermal Extrusion**:
   * Smelt or blast **[Plastic Pellets](Plastic-Pellets)** in a Furnace or Blast Furnace to form the consolidated **Plastic Block**.
