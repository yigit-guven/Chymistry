# Reinforced Glass Bottle

**Item ID:** `chymistry:reinforced_glass_bottle`

The **Reinforced Glass Bottle** is a reinforced alchemical container crafted from [Reinforced Glass](Reinforced-Glass) to safely hold corrosive mineral acids and volatile solvents.

## Mechanics

* **Acid Resistance**: Unlike regular glass bottles which cannot withstand corrosive reagents, Reinforced Glass Bottles can contain [Hydrochloric Acid](Hydrochloric-Acid), [Nitric Acid](Nitric-Acid), [Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid), [Gold Solvent](Gold-Solvent), and pure [Alcohol](Alcohol-Bottle).
* **In-World Placement**: Can be placed on the ground as a 3D block. When placed adjacent to an active **[Alembic](Alembic)**, the alembic automatically links its condenser pipe to the bottle to collect distilled chemical solutions.
* **Bottle Preservation**: Consuming or utilizing acid bottles returns an empty Reinforced Glass Bottle.
