# Sea Water Bucket

**Item ID:** `chymistry:sea_water_bucket`

The Sea Water Bucket is a fluid container item.

## Mechanics
* Acts identically to a standard Water Bucket.
* When used, it places standard Minecraft Water (`minecraft:water`).
* When used in crafting, it leaves an empty Bucket (`minecraft:bucket`) in the crafting grid.
* Max stack size is 1.
