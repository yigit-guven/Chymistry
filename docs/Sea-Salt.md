# Sea Salt

**Item ID:** `chymistry:sea_salt`

**Sea Salt** is an evaporated mineral salt harvested from natural oceans.

## Acquisition

Smelting a **[Sea Water Bucket](Sea-Water-Bucket)** in a standard Furnace yields Sea Salt and returns an empty Iron Bucket:

| Furnace Input | Cooking Time | Output | Remainder |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **[Sea Water Bucket](Sea-Water-Bucket)** | 10s (200t) | **1x Sea Salt** | 1x Iron Bucket |

---

## Uses

* **[Hydrochloric Acid](Hydrochloric-Acid)**: Distilled in an **[Alembic](Alembic)** with [Elixir of Vitriol](Elixir-of-Vitriol).
* **[Disinfectant](Disinfectant)**: Distilled with Alcohol and Quicklime.
* **[Storm Powder](Storm-Powder)**: Pulverized in a **[Mortar](Mortar)** alongside Niter Dust.
