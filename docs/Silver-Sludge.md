# Silver Sludge

**Item ID:** `chymistry:silver_sludge`

**Silver Sludge** is a dense argentiferous chemical precipitate obtained through acid leaching.

## Acquisition

Formed in a heated **[Crucible](Crucible)** by dissolving raw iron in nitric acid:

| Station | Inputs | Temperature Range | Time | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[Crucible](Crucible)** | 1x Raw Iron + 1x [Nitric Acid](Nitric-Acid) | 20 to 100 Heat | 10s (200t) | **1x Silver Sludge** + 1x Reinforced Bottle |

---

## Processing

* **Smelting**: Smelt or blast Silver Sludge in a Furnace or Blast Furnace to extract **1x [Silver Ingot](Silver-Ingot)**.
* **Crucible Refining**: Smelt in a **[Crucible](Crucible)** with **[Ash](Ash)** (30 to 150 Heat, 10s) to yield **1x [Silver Ingot](Silver-Ingot)**.
