# Plastic Pellets

**Item ID:** `chymistry:plastic_pellets`

**Plastic Pellets** are refined thermoplastic granules synthesized through the acid-catalyzed polymerization of hydrocarbon fuels.

## Acquisition

Synthesized in a **[Crucible](Crucible)**:

| Station | Inputs | Temperature Range | Time | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[Crucible](Crucible)** | 1x Coal + 1x [Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid) | 25 to 120 Heat | 10s (200t) | **2x Plastic Pellets** + 1x Reinforced Bottle |

---

## Processing

Smelt or blast Plastic Pellets in a Furnace or Blast Furnace to extrude **[Plastic Blocks](Plastic-Block)**.
