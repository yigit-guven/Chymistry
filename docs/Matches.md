# Matches

**Matches** are handheld dynamic lighting tools available in all 16 Minecraft dye colors.

## Mechanics

1. **Striking / Lighting**:
   * **Right-Click** in hand (or right-click with the match inside your inventory) to strike and light the match with a flint-and-steel spark.
   * Right-click again while lit to extinguish it.
2. **Mobile Dynamic Illumination**:
   * While lit and held, the match projects dynamic light following the player.
   * Emits floating colored spark particles matching its dye color.
3. **Burn Duration**:
   * A lit match burns for **60 seconds** (60 durability, consuming 1 durability per second).
   * Once fully burned down, the match extinguishes and is consumed.
