# Potion of Immunity

**Item ID:** `chymistry:immunity_potion`

The **Potion of Immunity** is a masterwork alchemical brew that shields the imbiber from all external physiological and magical afflictions.

## Effects

* **Consumption**: Drinking grants the **[Immunity](Immunity)** status effect for **3 minutes** (3:00).
* **Total Immunity**: Continuously and instantaneously purges all other status effects (Poison, Wither, Slowness, Weakness, Blindness, Darkness, Levitation, Bad Omen, etc.).
* Returns an empty Glass Bottle upon consumption.

---
