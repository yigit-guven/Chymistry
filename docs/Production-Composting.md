# Production Composting

**Production Composting** is the alchemical technique of chemically modifying the organic compost inside a full composter before it decomposes into standard bone meal.

## Process

When a standard vanilla Composter reaches **Level 7** (completely filled with organic waste, just before converting to bone meal), players can introduce reagents to divert the composting reaction into specialized chemical blocks:

---

## Composter Transformations & Recipes

### 1. Niter Soil Composting ➔ [Niter Dust](Niter-Dust)
* **Trigger:** Right-click a Level 7 Composter with **[Ash](Ash)**.
* The block transforms into a **[Niter Soil Composter](Niter-Soil-Composter)**.
* **Harvest:** Right-click the composter with an empty hand to extract **1x [Niter Dust](Niter-Dust)**. The composter resets to an empty vanilla composter.

---

### 2. Multi-Stage Composting ➔ [Super Fertilizer](Super-Fertilizer)
* **Trigger:** To a **[Niter Soil Composter](Niter-Soil-Composter)**, insert all three reagents:
  1. **[Ash](Ash)**
  2. **[Niter Dust](Niter-Dust)**
  3. **Bone Meal**
* Once all three reagents are placed inside, the composter emits celebratory particles and transitions into a Super Fertilizer composter.
* **Harvest:** Right-click with an empty hand to retrieve **1x [Super Fertilizer](Super-Fertilizer)**.
* **Utility:** Super Fertilizer acts as a hyper-potent plant growth accelerator, fertilizing all crops in an expansive **5x5 area** in a single use.

---

### 3. Solvation Composting ➔ Cyan Dye
* **Trigger:** Right-click a Level 7 Composter with an **[Alcohol Bottle](Alcohol-Bottle)** or **Raw Copper**.
* The block transforms into a **[Cyan Dye Composter](Cyan-Dye-Composter)**.
* Add the remaining ingredient so that both **1x Alcohol Bottle** (returns an empty Reinforced Glass Bottle) and **1x Raw Copper** are present.
* **Harvest:** Right-click with an empty hand to extract **2x Cyan Dye**.
