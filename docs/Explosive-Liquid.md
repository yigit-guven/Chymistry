# Liquid Explosive

**Item ID:** `chymistry:explosive_liquid`

**Liquid Explosive** (Nitroglycerin compound) is an ultra-sensitive, shock-detonating chemical liquid contained in a [Tinted Glass Bottle](Tinted-Glass-Bottle).

## Mechanics

* **Instant Detonation**: Stacks up to 16. Right-clicking hurls the bottle forward. Unlike [Dynamite](Dynamite), it features **zero fuse delay** and detonates instantaneously upon impact with any block or entity.
* **Demolition Compound**: Used to stabilize and craft delayed-fuse **[Dynamite](Dynamite)** with sand and string.

---

## Synthesis

Distilled in an **[Alembic](Alembic)** with an adjacent Tinted Glass Bottle:

| Station | Inputs | Time | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **[Alembic](Alembic)** | 1x Gunpowder + 1x [Nitric Acid](Nitric-Acid) + 1x [Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid) | 12s (240t) | **1x Liquid Explosive** (In Tinted Bottle) |
