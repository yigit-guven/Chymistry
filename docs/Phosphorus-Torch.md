# Phosphorus Torch

The **Phosphorus Torch** is a specialized chemical torch capable of burning underwater.

## Properties

* **Waterloggable**: Unlike standard torches which break immediately when submerged, Phosphorus Torches can be placed directly underwater or inside water source blocks.
* **Luminance**:
  * **Submerged / Waterlogged**: Emits maximum brightness (**Light Level 15**), illuminating ocean floors, flooded caves, and underwater structures.
  * **Dry / Air**: Emits subdued illumination (**Light Level 8**).
* **Placement**: Mountable on the ground (standing) or against walls (wall torch).
