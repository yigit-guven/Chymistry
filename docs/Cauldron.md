# Cauldron Chemistry

**Cauldron Chemistry** leverages standard water-filled cauldrons as alchemical submersion baths to induce chemical oxidation, hydration, and leaching.

## Mechanics

1. **Setup**: Fill a standard cauldron with water (must have at least the minimum required water level, typically Level 1).
2. **Submersion**:
   * Right-click the cauldron while holding the reactant item, or drop the item directly inside the cauldron basin.
   * The item rests centered in the liquid.
3. **Reaction**:
   * As the item reacts with the water, the cauldron continuously emits bubbling water particles and splashes.
4. **Completion**:
   * Once the processing timer elapses, the input item is consumed, the resulting product floats upward out of the cauldron, and the designated water level is deducted.
