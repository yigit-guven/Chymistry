# Soap Bleaching

**Soap Bleaching** is a chemical cleansing mechanic in Chymistry that strips artificial dyes and pigments from blocks and items, restoring them to their uncolored or white baseline.

## Mechanics

Soap bars possess **64 points of durability** and can be utilized in two ways:

### 1. In-World Cleansing
* **Right-Click**: Holding **[Soap](Soap)** and right-clicking any placed dyed block (Wool, Terracotta, Glazed Terracotta, Concrete, Concrete Powder, Stained Glass, Candles, Beds, Shulker Boxes) strips its dye and returns it to its white or natural state.
* Consumes 1 durability per block cleaned.

### 2. Crafting Grid Bleaching
* Combine **1x [Soap](Soap)** with any dyed item in any crafting grid.
* The recipe produces the bleached, undyed version of the item.
* The Soap remains in the crafting grid with its durability reduced by 1 point.

---

## Supported Materials

* **Wool & Carpets**: Cleans all 15 colored variants back to White Wool / White Carpet.
* **Terracotta & Glazed Terracotta**: Strips dyed pigments back to natural Terracotta or White Glazed Terracotta.
* **Concrete & Concrete Powder**: Reverts colors back to White Concrete / Powder.
* **Stained Glass & Panes**: Returns to clean transparent Glass.
* **Candles, Beds, & Shulker Boxes**: Reverts to White or undyed variants.
