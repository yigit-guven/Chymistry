# Incendiary Dust

**Item ID:** `chymistry:incendiary_dust`

**Incendiary Dust** is a volatile pyrotechnic fuel powder formulated from oxidized iron and sulfurous propellant.

## Acquisition

Ground in a **[Mortar & Pestle](Mortar)**:

| Station | Inputs | Presses | Speed | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[Mortar](Mortar)** | 1x [Rust Powder](Rust-Powder) + 1x Gunpowder | 4 | Any | **2x Incendiary Dust** |

---

## Uses

* **[Matches](Matches)**: Combined with a Stick and Dye in a crafting table to craft 8 colored matches.
* **Magma Blocks**: Pulverized with Stone in a **[Mortar](Mortar)** to synthesize Magma Blocks.
