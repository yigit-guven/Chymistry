# Nitric Acid

**Item ID:** `chymistry:nitric_acid_bottle`

**Nitric Acid** (Aqua Fortis) is a highly oxidizing and corrosive mineral acid contained in a [Reinforced Glass Bottle](Reinforced-Glass-Bottle).

## Uses

* **[Silver Sludge](Silver-Sludge)**: Reacted with Raw Iron in a **[Crucible](Crucible)** to leach silver.
* **[Gold Solvent](Gold-Solvent)**: Combined with [Hydrochloric Acid](Hydrochloric-Acid) in a **[Crucible](Crucible)**.
* **[Liquid Explosive](Explosive-Liquid)**: Distilled in an **[Alembic](Alembic)** alongside [Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid) and Gunpowder.
