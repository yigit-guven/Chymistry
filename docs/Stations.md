# Stations

Functional workstations used to process materials and synthesize alchemical reagents.

* <img src="images/blocks/mortarmodel.png" width="24" height="24" style="image-rendering: pixelated; vertical-align: middle;"> **[Mortar & Pestle](Mortar)**: Interactive pulverizing station for grinding solids and organic reagents.
* <img src="images/blocks/irontongs_carry_brickcrucible.png" width="24" height="24" style="image-rendering: pixelated; vertical-align: middle;"> **[Crucible](Crucible)**: High-temperature metallurgical basin with Brick, Deepslate, and Netherite variants.
* <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yigit-guven/Chymistry/26.2-neoforge/src/main/resources/assets/chymistry/textures/item/alcohol_bottle.png" width="24" height="24" style="image-rendering: pixelated; vertical-align: middle;"> **[Alembic](Alembic)**: Distillation apparatus for boiling and condensing liquids into adjacent bottles.
* <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yigit-guven/Chymistry/26.2-neoforge/src/main/resources/assets/chymistry/textures/item/rust_powder.png" width="24" height="24" style="image-rendering: pixelated; vertical-align: middle;"> **[Cauldron Chemistry](Cauldron)**: Utilizing fluid-filled cauldrons for chemical washing and conversions.
