# Storm Powder

**Item ID:** `chymistry:storm_powder`

## Acquisition

Ground in a **[Mortar & Pestle](Mortar)** with rapid clicking:

| Station | Inputs | Presses | Speed | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[Mortar](Mortar)** | 1x [Sea Salt](Sea-Salt) + 1x [Niter Dust](Niter-Dust) | 4 | Fast | **2x Storm Powder** |

---

## Usage

* **[Storm Ritual](Storm-Ritual)**: Added alongside **[Creosote Oil](Creosote-Oil)** into a **[Crucible](Crucible)** to initiate a localized 60-second rainstorm ritual under required thermal and biome conditions.
