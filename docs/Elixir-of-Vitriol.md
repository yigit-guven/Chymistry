# Elixir of Vitriol

**Item ID:** `chymistry:elixir_of_vitriol`

The Elixir of Vitriol is a consumable item. 

## Consumption Mechanics
Drinking the item triggers two status effects:
* **[Vitriol Immunity](Vitriol-Immunity):** Duration of 30 seconds (600 ticks).
* **Slowness:** Duration of 5 seconds (100 ticks).

## Item Properties
* Returns an empty **[Tinted Glass Bottle](Tinted-Glass-Bottle)** when consumed.
* Displays custom tooltip information showing the exact duration and modifier (-15% Movement Speed) of the applied effects.
* Item name renders in dark magenta (`0x8B008B`).
