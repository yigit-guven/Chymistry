# Thermometer

**Item ID:** `chymistry:thermometer`

The **Thermometer** is an alchemical diagnostic instrument used to measure temperatures and monitor thermodynamic processes.

## Mechanics

1. **HUD Heat Gauge Overlay**:
   * Holding a Thermometer in either your main hand or off-hand projects a real-time HUD thermal overlay on your screen.
   * Looking at any **[Crucible](Crucible)** displays its exact heat rating, safe operating window, and target temperature.
2. **Crucible GUI Readout**:
   * Carrying a Thermometer in your inventory unlocks the numerical temperature readout directly inside the Crucible's container interface.
3. **Tongs Integration**:
   * Having a Thermometer in your inventory reveals the internal temperature of any crucible held in your **[Iron Tongs](Iron-Tongs)** tooltip.
