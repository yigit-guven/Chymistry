# Green Crystal

**Item ID:** `chymistry:green_crystal`

The **Green Crystal** (also known in early alchemy as *Green Vitriol* / Ferrous Sulfate) is a crystallized mineral salt synthesized from copper and iron dusts.

## Crafting

Synthesized shapelessly in any crafting grid:

| Station | Ingredients | Output |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Crafting Table** | 2x [Iron Dust](Iron-Dust) + 2x [Copper Dust](Copper-Dust) | **2x Green Crystal** |

---

## Uses

* **[Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid)**: Distilled in an **[Alembic](Alembic)** with an adjacent [Reinforced Glass Bottle](Reinforced-Glass-Bottle) to yield Sulfuric Acid and [Rust Powder](Rust-Powder).
