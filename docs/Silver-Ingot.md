## Acquisition

1. **Smelting Precipitate**:
   * Smelt or blast **[Silver Sludge](Silver-Sludge)** in a standard Furnace or Blast Furnace.
2. **Crucible Reduction**:
   * Combine 1x [Silver Sludge](Silver-Sludge) and 1x [Ash](Ash) in a **[Crucible](Crucible)** between 30 to 150 Heat.
