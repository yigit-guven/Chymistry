# Vigor

**Effect ID:** `chymistry:vigor`

**Vigor** is a beneficial physiological status effect that rapidly regenerates the entity's health pool.

## Mechanics

* **Proportional Healing**: Unlike standard Regeneration which heals fixed health points, Vigor restores **5% of the entity's maximum health** every 10 ticks (0.5 seconds).
* Over its standard 10-second duration, Vigor regenerates up to **100% of maximum health**.
* **Source**: Granted by consuming **[Vigor Potion](Vigor-Potion)**.
