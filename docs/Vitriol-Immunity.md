# Vitriol Immunity

**Effect ID:** `chymistry:vitriol_immunity`

Vitriol Immunity is a custom status effect (Mob Effect) registered by the mod.

## Source
This effect is currently only obtainable by consuming the [Elixir of Vitriol](Elixir-of-Vitriol).

## Mechanics
While active, Vitriol Immunity protects the entity from specific forms of damage related to acidic chemical processes (implemented elsewhere in the mod).
