# Wood Treatment

**Wood Treatment** is an alchemical preservation process that permanently confers complete fire immunity to wooden building blocks.

## Mechanics

Wood treatment infuses lumber with **[Creosote Oil](Creosote-Oil)** (distilled in an **[Alembic](Alembic)** from Coal and Logs):

1. **Crafting**:
   * Arrange 8 identical wooden blocks (Planks, Slabs, Stairs, Fences, Fence Gates, Doors, Trapdoors, Buttons, or Pressure Plates) surrounding **1x [Creosote Oil](Creosote-Oil)** in a 3x3 crafting grid.
   * Produces **8x Treated** variants of the wooden component and returns the empty Glass Bottle.

| Grid | Inputs | Result |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Crafting Table (3x3)** | Center: 1x [Creosote Oil](Creosote-Oil)<br>Ring: 8x Any Planks / Wooden Block | **8x Treated Wood** + 1x Glass Bottle |

2. **Properties**:
   * **Fireproof**: When placed in the world, Treated Wood blocks can never catch fire, burn away, or spread flame. Lava and fire will not consume them.
   * **Enchantment Glint**: Treated wood items carry a subtle protective sheen and "Treated" prefix in their tooltip.
   * **Drop Preservation**: Breaking a placed treated block properly yields its Treated Wood item form.
