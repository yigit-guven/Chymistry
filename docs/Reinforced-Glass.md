# Reinforced Glass

**Reinforced Glass** is an explosion-resistant, chemically inert structural glass imbued with powdered iron.

## Properties

* **Blast Resistance**: Engineered with a blast resistance rating of **1200.0F**, making it entirely immune to explosions from TNT, Creepers, and the Wither.
* **Aesthetics**: Retains transparent glass visibility while displaying a distinctive holographic protective glint.

---

## Reinforced Glass Bottles

Standard glass bottles shatter or dissolve when exposed to strong mineral acids and organic solvents. Crafting **[Reinforced Glass Bottles](Reinforced-Glass-Bottle)** provides the acid-proof containment required for:
* **[Hydrochloric Acid](Hydrochloric-Acid)**
* **[Nitric Acid](Nitric-Acid)**
* **[Sulfuric Acid](Sulfuric-Acid)**
* **[Gold Solvent](Gold-Solvent)**
* **[Alcohol Bottle](Alcohol-Bottle)**

### Placement
* Reinforced Glass Bottles can be placed directly on the ground as 3D blocks.
* When placed adjacent to an **[Alembic](Alembic)**, they serve as heavy-duty condensation vessels to capture corrosive distillates.
