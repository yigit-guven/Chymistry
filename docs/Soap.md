# Soap

**Item ID:** `chymistry:soap`

**Soap** is a crafted bar of alkali-saponified detergent used in [Soap Bleaching](Soap-Bleaching).

</div>

## Properties

* **Durability**: 64 uses.
* **Function**: Strips dyes and pigments from blocks and items, restoring them to white or natural states.

---

## Usage

* **In-World**: Right-click any placed dyed block (Wool, Concrete, Terracotta, Glass, Shulker Boxes, Candles) to bleach it directly.
* **Crafting**: Place Soap and any dyed item into a crafting grid to extract the bleached item while preserving the soap (minus 1 durability).

---

## Synthesis

Saponified in a heated **[Crucible](Crucible)**:

| Station | Inputs | Temperature Range | Time | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[Crucible](Crucible)** | 1x [Animal Fat](Animal-Fat) + 1x [Quicklime](Quicklime) + 1x Water Bucket | 10 to 60 Heat | 5s (100t) | **1x Soap** + 1x Bucket |
