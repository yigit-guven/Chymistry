# Super Fertilizer

**Item ID:** `chymistry:super_fertilizer`

**Super Fertilizer** is an enriched alchemical plant stimulant designed for large-scale agricultural acceleration.

## Mechanics

* **Area-of-Effect Growth**: Right-clicking any crop, sapling, or grass block with Super Fertilizer applies the bone meal acceleration effect to all viable vegetation across a **5x5 square area** (radius 2) centered on the clicked position.
* Emits sparkling green villager particles across the entire treated patch.

---

## Acquisition

Produced via [Production Composting](Production-Composting):
1. Right-click a Level 7 Composter with **[Ash](Ash)** to create a **[Niter Soil Composter](Niter-Soil-Composter)**.
2. Insert **[Niter Dust](Niter-Dust)** and **Bone Meal**.
3. Right-click with an empty hand to harvest **1x Super Fertilizer**.
