# Defoliant Potion

**Item ID:** `chymistry:defoliant_potion`

**Defoliant Potion** is an aggressive chemical herbicide engineered for rapid large-scale land clearing and vegetation removal.

## Mechanics

* **Application**: Right-click any soil block (Dirt, Grass, Farmland, or Dirt Path) with Defoliant Potion in hand.
* **Area Clearing**:
  * Instantly withers and removes all leaves, tall grass, flowers, vines, bushes, and agricultural crops in an expansive **20x20x20 cubic volume** (radius 10) centered on the clicked position.
  * Preserves underlying logs and dirt while stripping away all obstructive foliage.
  * Consumes the potion and returns an empty [Tinted Glass Bottle](Tinted-Glass-Bottle).
