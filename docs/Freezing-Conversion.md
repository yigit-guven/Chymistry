# Freezing Conversion

**Freezing Conversion** is an endothermic phase-change reaction enabled by **[Freezing Powder](Freezing-Powder)**.

## Mechanics

When Freezing Powder is right-clicked on an active liquid source block, it induces an instant hyper-cooling crystallization reaction:

| Target Fluid Source | Transformed Block | Reaction Dynamics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water Source** | **Blue Ice** | Instantly crystallizes liquid water into dense Blue Ice with snowflake particles and cracking sounds. |
| **Lava Source** | **Obsidian** | Rapidly quenches molten lava into hard Obsidian with heavy smoke dissipation. |

Each conversion consumes 1x Freezing Powder (unless in Creative mode).

---

## Synthesis

Freezing Powder is synthesized by cooling volatile reactants in a cryogenic **[Crucible](Crucible)** resting above Ice or Blue Ice:

| Station | Inputs | Temperature Range | Time | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[Crucible](Crucible)** | 4x Snowballs + 1x [Niter Dust](Niter-Dust) | -64 to 0 Heat | 10s (200t) | **2x Freezing Powder** |
| **[Crucible](Crucible)** | 1x Snow Block + 1x [Niter Dust](Niter-Dust) | -64 to 0 Heat | 10s (200t) | **2x Freezing Powder** |
